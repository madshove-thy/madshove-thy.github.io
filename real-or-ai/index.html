Real or AI?

You will see seven images, one at a time. Some are AI-generated, some are authentic photographs.

  • All images are meant to show real events. Each is either an authentic photograph of the event or an AI-generated image of it.
  • Imagine you saw the image while scrolling on social media, posted by someone you do not know.
  • For each image, rate how likely it is that it was generated with AI, from 0 (certainly authentic) to 100 (certainly AI-generated).
  • Give your immediate impression. About five seconds per image. Please do not talk to your neighbors.

Anonymous: no names or accounts. Your seven ratings, how long you looked at each image and a random session code are saved for class use only.
